# E-Commerce-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard
Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing large-scale E-Commerce sales data using Power BI to uncover business insights, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard was developed using transactional sales data containing over 2 million records. It provides a comprehensive view of revenue performance, order trends, customer behavior, product category performance, and payment preferences.

The goal of this project is to help business stakeholders identify growth opportunities, improve operational efficiency, and optimize sales performance.

Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of sales data daily. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to:

Track overall business performance
Monitor revenue growth
Identify top-performing product categories
Understand customer purchasing behavior
Optimize payment and sales strategies
Detect declining trends and business risks

This dashboard addresses these challenges by converting raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

Key Insights
Insight 1

A small number of product categories generated a significant portion of overall revenue, indicating strong customer demand concentration.

Insight 2

Monthly sales trends revealed seasonal fluctuations, highlighting opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns during peak periods.

Insight 3

Certain payment methods were preferred by customers and contributed to a higher percentage of successful transactions.

Insight 4

A limited group of products consistently outperformed others, suggesting potential opportunities for focused inventory and promotional strategies.

Insight 5

Customer purchasing behavior indicated opportunities for cross-selling and upselling complementary products.

Business Recommendations
1. Focus on High-Performing Categories

Increase inventory availability and promotional activities for top-performing categories to maximize revenue growth.

2. Improve Low-Performing Product Segments

Analyze underperforming products and implement targeted pricing, bundling, or marketing strategies.

3. Leverage Seasonal Demand

Plan inventory and marketing campaigns based on seasonal sales patterns identified through trend analysis.

4. Optimize Payment Experience

Promote the most preferred payment methods to improve customer experience and transaction success rates.

5. Enhance Customer Retention

Implement loyalty programs and personalized recommendations based on customer purchasing behavior.
